Our Manifesto

Nobody's life is a constant. Nobody has every answer. Nobody knows when it's the right time.

But everybody does have a guide. Ever since the first human, we have all looked upon the same stars — guided by the brightest one — wondering where our place is within it all.

It's way too easy to get caught up in our busy lives. Each time wondering where the week went. Far too often just finding ourselves working until the weekend on repeat.

I can't tell you where your place is within all of this. But I can ask — what's your north star, and how are you going to find it?

We built LIFE for that question. Not to answer it, but to help you explore it.

I grew up in a rural town, one where I found the most amazement in the way the sun would set over the corn fields. Not demanding presence, but just subtly making it known. It was moments like those where I found the most understanding of my life.

When I was sitting next to a bonfire looking up at the huge moon in the sky surrounded by what looked like millions of stars, that's where I understood I was meant for something great.

I just didn't realize until now that it would be this.

Finding this understanding doesn't require a trust fund, a gap year, or the right gear. All it asks is one decision, one weekend — not just to go somewhere, but to embrace it. To fall in love with it.

LIFE is for the spontaneous decision made on a Thursday night. The two-hour drive to nowhere. The trail you'd never heard of that changed something in you. The moment you stopped waiting and just went.

The right time is right now.
It always has been.

— LIFE
